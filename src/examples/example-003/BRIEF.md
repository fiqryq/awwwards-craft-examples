# Lengkung — creative brief

Concept:      Lengkung ("arch") designs houses as a sequence of thresholds; the site is walked, not scrolled — every important moment is entered through an arch-shaped doorway that opens into the next room.
Mood:         quiet, tactile, slow, warm-in-grey, confident
Audience:     Families and cultural clients in Java planning a house, a prayer house or a small public building; they should feel the studio's calm and book a site visit.
Palette:      #2 — paper #E0DEE1 (lavender-grey, one-color system) · ink #0F0F0F. Photography carries the warmth; dark sections invert to ink / paper.
Type:         Fontshare Boska Medium (+ italic, sentence case) + General Sans Light/Regular. Labels in General Sans uppercase, tabular numbers.
Grid:         12 col, 1.5rem gutters, max 1800px, single breakpoint 1000px.
Signature:    "Thresholds" — a pinned scene (desktop, 4 rooms × 1.4 viewports): an arch opens in the middle of the screen, you walk through it as it grows to fill the viewport, and the next arch opens inside that room. A paper caption card names each room. Mobile + reduced motion: the rooms stacked as arch-topped images.
Sections:     Preloader → Hero (full-bleed photo + overlaid type) → Practice statement (word fill on scroll) → Thresholds (pinned) → Selected work (index rows + cursor-follow preview) → Process (sticky heading, four steps) → Footer (CTA, giant wordmark, Yogyakarta clock).
Preloader:    A small arch rises in the center and flicks through five project photos while a counter runs 000 → 100; the wordmark exits and the arch expands to full screen, becoming the hero photo.
Menu:         Circle clip that grows from the menu toggle; serif links rise in masked lines.
Transition:   None (single page).
Imagery:      Unsplash architecture photography (see below), shown at strict ratios (4:5 rows, full-bleed hero, arch windows). Swap for the studio's own photos.
Motion notes: Slow and deliberate: hop / glide eases, 1–1.5s moves, nothing bouncy. Only the thresholds scene is scrubbed; everything else is a one-shot masked reveal.
