# Bayang — creative brief

Concept:      Bayang ("shadow") shows only what light leaves behind, so the site behaves like a darkroom: works arrive as negatives and are developed into positives as they cross the light.
Mood:         stark, hushed, exact, cinematic, generous
Audience:     Collectors, students and weekend visitors in Bandung who follow photography and print; they should feel the curatorial rigour and plan a visit or join the opening.
Palette:      #4 — pure white #FFFFFF + pure black #000000. No greys as surfaces; grey only from the photographs themselves. Sections invert hard.
Type:         Fontshare Gambarino Regular (display serif, sentence case) + Supreme Light/Regular/Medium (body, uppercase labels).
Grid:         12 col, 1.25rem gutters, max 1920px, single breakpoint 1000px.
Signature:    "Develop" — a pinned horizontal strip (desktop): seven works travel right → left past a fixed vertical line of light in the middle of the screen. Right of the line the page is a negative (black ground, inverted prints); left of it everything is developed into a positive. Mobile + reduced motion: a vertical list of positives.
Sections:     Preloader → Hero (split headline with an inset print) → Now showing (exhibition statement + credits) → Develop (pinned) → Programme (rows that invert on hover) → Artists (marquee) → Visit (live open/closed status, hours) → Footer (giant wordmark, clock).
Preloader:    SVG stroke draws the gallery mark (a square aperture with a circle) in uneven steps, then a two-tone wipe (black then white) lifts off the hero.
Menu:         The page shell slides down and away to reveal a black menu underneath; serif links rise in masked lines.
Transition:   None (single page).
Imagery:      Unsplash photography converted to true greyscale with a gentle S-curve (see list). Strict 4:5 and 3:4 ratios.
Motion notes: Unhurried and exact: hop/glide eases, masked line reveals, no bounce. The only scrubbed moment is Develop; hovers are hard inversions, not fades.
