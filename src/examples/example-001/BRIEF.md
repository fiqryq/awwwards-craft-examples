# Firstcrack — creative brief

Concept:      Every roast is a curve. The page reads like a roast in progress: scroll heats the bean, and the site's signature moment is drawing a live roast curve from charge to drop.
Mood:         precise, warm, confident, crafted, calm-under-heat
Audience:     Owners and head roasters at small-batch specialty roasteries (1–15 kg roasters). They should feel "this was built by people who roast" and start a free trial.
Palette:      #7 — paper #FDF2DE (cream) · ink #3B60C5 (cornflower blue). Dark sections invert to blue background / cream text. Deep text #0A0A0A only for long body copy on cream. No brown — avoids the coffee cliché.
Type:         Fontshare pair Cabinet Grotesk Extrabold + Satoshi Medium (neo-grotesk, SaaS-crisp) + JetBrains Mono for live readouts (technical concept).
Grid:         12 col, 2rem gutters, max 2000px, single breakpoint 1000px.
Signature:    "The Roast" — a pinned scene (desktop) where scroll draws the bean-temperature curve through Drying → Maillard → First crack → Development → Drop, with live readouts (BT °C, RoR, elapsed time) and phase labels swapping. Mobile: static annotated curve.
Sections:     Preloader → Hero (giant headline + product dashboard mock) → Roastery marquee → The Roast (pinned) → Modules (4 sticky stacked cards: Profiles, Green inventory, Production planner, Cupping & QC) → Numbers → Pricing (3 tiers) → Testimonial → CTA → Footer (wordmark + live clock).
Preloader:    Charge temperature counter 020° → 205° (first crack), a progress bar that grows then exits the other way, then the panel clips away upward into the hero.
Menu:         Inline links on desktop; on mobile a full overlay clipped in from the bottom.
Transition:   None (single page).
Imagery:      No photography needed — product UI mocks built in HTML/CSS/SVG (dashboard, curve, inventory table). Keeps it crisp and fast.
Motion notes: Calm and precise. Masked line reveals everywhere; the only loud moment is The Roast. Readouts count with tabular numerals. Exits faster than entrances.
