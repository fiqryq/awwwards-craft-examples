# Sinyal — creative brief

Concept:      The festival is a radio band. You don't browse the line-up, you tune into it: scrolling turns the dial, and each stage is a station that locks in out of the static.
Mood:         loud, graphic, nocturnal, analogue, hopeful
Audience:     18–35 Jakarta crowd who follow club nights and live electronics; they should feel the edition is curated and buy a pass before phase 2 sells out.
Palette:      #6 — paper #E5CF4F (mustard) · ink #190BB6 (ultramarine). The tuner, menu and footer invert to blue background / mustard text.
Type:         Fontshare Khand Bold (condensed poster, uppercase) + Switzer Regular · DM Mono for frequencies, times and labels.
Grid:         12 col, 1.5rem gutters, max 2000px, single breakpoint 1000px.
Signature:    "Tune in" — a pinned scene (desktop, 6 viewports): a frequency scale slides under a fixed needle from 87.5 to 108.0 MHz. Between stations the readout scrambles and the waveform is noise; at 89.1 / 93.7 / 98.3 / 104.5 the dial locks, the waveform turns into a clean sine and that stage's line-up rises in. Mobile + reduced motion: the four stations as a stacked list.
Sections:     Preloader → Hero (type-only, live countdown) → Artist marquee (scroll-reactive) → Tune in (pinned) → Line-up by night → Passes (ticket stubs) → The harbour (info + FAQ) → Footer (CTA, giant wordmark, live clock).
Preloader:    Grid of ultramarine blocks covers the screen while a frequency counter sweeps 87.5 → 98.3 MHz ("searching signal"), then the blocks collapse (scaleY → 0, random stagger) onto the hero.
Menu:         Toggle on every breakpoint; three colored layers drop in from the top (stagger 0.1), then links rise on the top layer.
Transition:   None (single page).
Imagery:      None — type, SVG dial and waveform carry the page.
Motion notes: Punchy and quick. Flicker and scramble for anything "broadcast", masked slides for copy. The tuner is the only scrubbed moment; everything else is a one-shot reveal.
